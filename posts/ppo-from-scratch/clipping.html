r·A (unclipped) LCLIP = min(r·A, clip·A)

LCLIP(r) = min(r·A, clip(r, 1−ε, 1+ε)·A) as a function of the probability ratio r = πθ/πθ_old. Slide A and watch the asymmetry. When A > 0, the upper clip caps the gain — fine. When A < 0, you might expect the lower clip to do the symmetric job, but the min picks the worse of the two terms: if r drifts past 1+ε while A < 0, we get the unclipped (deeply negative) penalty rather than letting the clip mask it. The min is the whole point — it's why bad updates don't get a free pass.
