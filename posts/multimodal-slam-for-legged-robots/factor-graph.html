A factor graph for a short trajectory, built incrementally as sensor measurements arrive. Each circle is a variable to estimate; each colored line is a factor — a residual that ties variables together with a covariance. Drag the slider through the timeline (or hit Play). The optimizer's job is to find variable values that jointly minimize the squared residuals across every factor present.
