SO(2)   (Lie group)

The Lie algebra 𝔰𝔬(2) is a real line; the Lie group SO(2) is the unit circle. The exponential map ω ↦ R(ω) wraps the line onto the circle. Slide ω: values 2π apart map to the same rotation — the map is many-to-one. The faint polyline on the right is the limit definition (I + ωJ/n)k·e1 for k = 0…n; at n = 1 it lands far from the circle, and as n grows it straightens onto the true arc. Compound interest, made visible.
