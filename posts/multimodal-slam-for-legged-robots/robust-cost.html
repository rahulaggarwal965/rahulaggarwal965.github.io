L2 Huber Cauchy

A measurement's influence on the optimization is the slope of its cost: it tells you how hard a residual r pulls on the variables. L2 says every residual pulls in proportion to its size, so a single huge residual pulls the hardest of all — exactly what you do not want from a known-bad measurement. Huber and Cauchy cap or shrink that influence beyond a threshold δ. The optimizer still listens to small residuals normally; it just stops letting one wild measurement steamroll the rest.
