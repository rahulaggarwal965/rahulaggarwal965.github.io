Sphere (drag to rotate)

Chart φ(U) ⊂ ℝ²

A chart is a map from a patch of a manifold to a patch of flat space. Here the manifold is the unit sphere, the patch is a spherical cap of angular radius α around the marked point, and the chart is orthogonal projection onto the tangent plane at that point. Drag the sphere to slide the chart center to a different spot. Shrink α: the projected lat/long lines become indistinguishable from straight lines — that is what "locally Euclidean" means in pictures.
