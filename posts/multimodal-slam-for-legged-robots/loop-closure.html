Loop closure as a loose-coupling pipeline. The robot's current frame triggers a fast nearest-neighbor search in a stored bank of visual features; the search returns several plausibly-similar past frames, but visual similarity is not enough — two corridors can look identical. A geometric check (GICP between the lidar scans) filters the false positives, and only the surviving relative-pose estimate is handed to the optimizer as a single factor with a tight covariance. The factor graph never sees the candidate list, the feature vectors, or the rejected scans. That separation is what loose coupling buys you: a complex front-end behind a simple interface.
