A robot at unknown pose T ∈ SE(2) takes noisy measurements bj of landmarks in its body frame. Filled dots are the world-frame landmarks Lj (known). Open circles are the current estimate's predictions T·bj — where the algorithm thinks the landmarks would land if its pose guess were right. Lines are residuals. Each Gauss-Newton step linearizes the residuals in local coordinates ξ ∈ ℝ³, solves a 3×3 linear system, and retracts via T ← T·exp(ξ̂). Convergence is quadratic once the estimate is close to the truth.
