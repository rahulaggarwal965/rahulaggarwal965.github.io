After self-attention

Eight vertex embeddings drawn in a 2D feature space. The left panel shows the inputs; the right shows the output of one self-attention pass with Q = K = V = inputs. At high τ the attention weights are nearly uniform and every output collapses toward the centroid. At low τ each vertex attends almost only to itself — nothing changes. In between, the attention pulls each vertex toward the centroid of its nearest cluster, the noisy outliers drift toward the cluster they're closest to, and the spread tightens. That is, in essence, what we want a denoising step on a noisy semantic neighborhood to do. Drag any input point to see the effect propagate.
