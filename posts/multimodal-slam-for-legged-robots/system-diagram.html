The ROS-flavoured architecture, with topic names on the wires and the TF tree drawn out at the bottom. Each sensor lives in its own process; a front-end stage condenses raw measurements into a single factor with a covariance, and the factor graph back-end (iSAM2) consumes them as they arrive. Most front-ends are loosely coupled — they hand a relative-pose estimate to the optimizer and nothing else; only joint kinematics enters the residual directly. The IMU pulls double duty: it produces a preintegration factor like everyone else, and its 200 Hz pose feeds the lidar front-end so each scan's points can be motion-compensated (the dashed orange "deskew" link), and it publishes the high-rate odom → base transform on the TF tree.
