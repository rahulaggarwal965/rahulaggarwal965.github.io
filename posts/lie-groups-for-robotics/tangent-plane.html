Tp S² — tangent plane at a point on the sphere (drag p)

The tangent space at a point is the linear space that best approximates the manifold there. For a sphere embedded in ℝ³ this is literally the plane tangent to the sphere at p. Drag p around the visible hemisphere — the plane rotates to stay tangent. The two arrows e₁, e₂ are an orthonormal basis of Tp S² ≅ ℝ²; every tangent vector at p is a linear combination of them.
