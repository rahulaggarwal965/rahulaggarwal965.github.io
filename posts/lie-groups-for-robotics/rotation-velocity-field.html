A grid of points with their velocity vectors under angular velocity ω. The velocity at each point p is ω·J·p = ω·(−py, px) — perpendicular to p, with magnitude proportional to the distance from the origin. Drag anywhere to highlight a point and see its velocity vector emphasized. This is the geometric content of the formula F(R, p) = R[−p]× for SO(3): each point's velocity is perpendicular to its position vector. Slide ω: arrows reverse and scale linearly. The pattern is the velocity field every rotating rigid body has.
