The full training loop. Each posed RGB image goes through the off-the-shelf 2D model, producing per-view labels that disagree with each other. AttentioNGP fits the disagreements into a single 3D-consistent field. Re-rendering that field at the training poses gives a cleaner set of pseudo-labels. Cross-entropy fine-tuning pushes the 2D model toward those pseudo-labels — the 3D consistency leaks back into the 2D weights, no new ground truth required.
