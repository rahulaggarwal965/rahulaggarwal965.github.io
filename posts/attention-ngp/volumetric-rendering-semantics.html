A side view of a tiny scene with two soft "objects" — a chair (orange) and a couch (blue). The camera fires a single ray from the left. At every sample point along the ray we read off two things: a density σ and a semantic distribution s (the bars below the ray). The familiar volumetric-rendering integral folds those samples into a single pixel: each sample's contribution is weighted by wn = Tn·αn, the product of "the ray hasn't been absorbed yet" and "the ray gets absorbed here." The final composited class distribution sits to the right. Slide the ray's pitch — the same scene resolves to whichever object the ray actually punches through.
