A simulated indoor scene rendered from a moving camera. The right panel shows what an off-the-shelf 2D segmentation network labels each object. The chair drifts between armchair, chair, and ottoman as the camera moves; the couch flips between couch and sofa; the wall colour changes once the model decides the surface is part of a different room.
