A planar quadruped leg with two joint angles (α1, α2) and a body pose (x, θ). The forward-kinematic function f(α) gives the foot position in the body frame; the world-frame foot is R(θ)·f(α) + p. When the foot is in rigid contact, the world-frame point c is fixed. Any pose that shifts the body without re-grounding moves the predicted foot away from c — the gap is the residual rF = R·f(α) + p − c. Plant the foot, then drag the body sliders to see the constraint pull.
